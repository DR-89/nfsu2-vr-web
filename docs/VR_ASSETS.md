# Browser cockpit assets

The `web/src/static/vr-*` meshes/textures come from the authored cockpit and
driver gloves in the native mod's `assets/cockpits/runtime/cockpits` directory.
They contain no extracted retail car geometry or textures. The cockpit is one
shared interior fitted to each vehicle, not a collection of authentic factory
interiors. Anonymous helmet heads are generated by the renderer.

Regenerate with Python and Pillow:

```text
python tools/pack_vr_assets.py <native-mod-root> <web-project-root>
```

The converter reads `shared.ncpk` v4 and `driver-gloves.nghv` v1, keeps their
authored geometry in metres, keeps matching open/grip glove vertices and reduces each
atlas to at most 1024 pixels per side. Vehicle measurements come from the native
mod's `quest_cockpit_vehicle.h`. The converter also generates matching C seat
positions (`runtime/web/vr_seats.h`), right-window fits, and wheel vertex/index ranges and pivot/axis
(`web/src/vr/cabin-parts.ts`), so camera and mesh fitting use the same data. No paths or source project identifiers are
embedded in the browser assets.

NVWM v1: 16-byte little-endian header (`NVWM`, version, vertex count, index count),
then 32-byte position/normal/UV vertices and unsigned 32-bit triangle indices.
Textures are separate PNGs. The browser validates sizes, finite vertices and
index bounds before creating GPU buffers. Left gloves mirror the right glove.

NVWM v2 adds a second 32-byte vertex array after the indices, with the same
vertex count and UVs. The glove uses its grip vertices as the base and open
vertices as a GPU morph target. The native `quest_glove_point_pose` index mask
extends the index while leaving the other fingers curled. The converter writes
the matching physical index pad to `web/src/vr/glove-tip.ts`. Local poses follow
control ownership (free, held, pointing); remote curl/index weights interpolate
between packets. Older v1 assets and packets retain their grip pose.

Remote interiors use the world's depth attachment, once for the main view and
again for each XR eye. Their visibility follows actual free-roam and LAN/Online
race car slots; no avatar-supplied world transform or car identifier is trusted.
Only the nearest 16 remote cars within 45 metres receive these additional meshes.

Cockpit instrument graphics come from live PC frontend draws, not extracted
images. The native gauge, map, windshield and side-window mounts sample a
transparent 1280x960 capture. PC-specific crops retain the turbo dial and the
map's 75-unit radius around its (97,382) frontend anchor. The local cabin's
wheel index range draws after the gauge, so the instrument can cover baked
trim while the physical rim and hands still occlude it.
The map and GPS strip share the live placement transform. GPS glyphs come from
the native 5x7 bitmap font, exported by the same asset converter; destination
distance and units come from checked PC GPS/HUD state. No game-derived text
texture or system font is needed for this strip.

The local cabin follows the native renderer's final pass with a separate depth
attachment, replacing the stock low-detail dashboard. The game's own car pass
retains its hood, pillars, roof and body. Only the local main-view rigid shell
gets two-sided rendering and transparent-window filtering. Window identities
come from the car's seven validated TextureInfo replacements and the stages
actually sampled by its pixel shader (PC car shaders use stage 1 for glass and
stage 0 for reflections). Mirrors, exterior/photo views and other cars keep
their materials. No retail body geometry is included in the browser asset pack.

## Optional community packs

`tools/prepare_vr_mods.py <native-mod-root> <prepared-PC-data>` imports the
private PC source packs retained by the native project. Use `--check` to verify
without writing. `tools/start-vr-web.ps1` accepts `-ModSource <native-mod-root>`
for the same import after preparing the PC data.

The importer validates NTXP HUD/maps, HQV vinyls, the English subtitle language
file, PCM whine samples and the crash scratch TPK through `tools/packcheck.py`.
It checks the restored PC soundtrack's event graph, stream offsets, codec and
buffer-sizing pair before replacing its two music files. The retail files stay
in `pfdata/original`, which the website's Original choice selects. Repeated
imports are idempotent; modified owned files are rejected. Routine PC data
preparation retains the verified imported soundtrack. Source paths and data
remain private; converted Xbox NHQ, geometry and sound-bank files are not
copied into the PC game.

Local acceptance verified 39 pack files and 43 soundtrack entries. The actual
career with HUD/maps, HQ vinyls, whine, stance and damage enabled rendered 498
replacement textures and four car skins, loaded all three whine loops, and
exited normally with zero GPU errors. This does not imply every car, damage
state or language has been visually tested.

The retail EA TRAX playlist is the default, matching the native mod. An explicit
Uncensored setting is preserved. Actual Chrome playback of the retained retail
files confirms the `pfdata/original` requests and nonzero decoded music PCM
(44.1 kHz), with zero GPU errors and a normal game exit.
